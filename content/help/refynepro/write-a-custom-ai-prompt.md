The five built-in actions cover common jobs. When you need something specific — translate this,
turn it into bullet points, make it fit a word count — write your own instruction.

## Steps

1. Select the text you want to work on.
2. Click **AI Tools**, then **Custom**.
3. Type your instruction. Be specific about what you want, as in *Rewrite this as three bullet
   points for a slide*.

   ![The custom prompt box with an instruction typed in](2026-08-09-22-21-54.png)

4. Press **Enter**, or click **Generate**. Then choose **Replace**, **Insert Below** or
   **Discard**, exactly as with the built-in actions.

Your instruction can be up to 1000 characters. Press **Shift+Enter** for a new line inside the
box, or **Esc** to cancel.

> **Tip:** Custom prompts are used once and not saved. If you have an instruction you rely on
> often, keep it in a RefynePro document and copy it in when you need it.
