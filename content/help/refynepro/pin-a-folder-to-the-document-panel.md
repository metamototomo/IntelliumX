Pinning lifts a folder out of the tree and puts it at the very top of the Document Panel, so you can
reach it without expanding anything.

## Steps

1. Right-click the folder in the Document Tree.
2. Choose **Pin to Document Panel**.

   ![A pinned folder shown under the Pinned heading at the top of the Document Panel](2026-08-09-21-51-27.png)

3. The folder now appears under a **Pinned** heading at the top. Click it any time to jump
   straight there.

To remove one, right-click the folder again and choose **Unpin from Document Panel**.

> **Tip:** Pins are remembered by this browser on this computer, not by your account. If you sign
> in somewhere else, you will need to pin your folders again there.

Only folders can be pinned. For quick access to a single document, use favourites instead.
