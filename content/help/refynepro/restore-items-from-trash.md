Deleted documents and folders go to the trash and stay there for **7 days**. After that they are
removed permanently and cannot be recovered.

## Steps

1. Click **Trash** in the Document Shortcuts.
2. Find the item you want. Each row shows when you deleted it and how many days remain before it
   is removed permanently.

   ![The trash window listing deleted items with Restore and Delete Forever buttons](2026-08-09-20-47-43.png)

3. Click **Restore** to put it back where it came from.

Restoring a folder brings back everything that was inside it, and recreates any parent folders it
needs along the way.

## Clearing the trash

**Delete Forever** removes a single item immediately. **Empty Trash** removes everything. Both
ask you to confirm, and neither can be undone.

> **Tip:** If you are on the Free plan and have hit the document limit, items in the trash still
> count. Use **Delete Forever** to genuinely free up space.
