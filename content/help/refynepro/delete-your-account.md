Deleting your account schedules it for permanent removal. You get 30 days to change your mind
before anything is actually erased.

## Before you start

If you have a paid plan, cancel it first using **Manage Subscription**. RefynePro will not delete
an account with an active subscription.

## Steps

1. Click the **person** icon in the Header to open the Account Panel, then scroll to the bottom.
2. Under **Danger Zone**, click **Delete Account**.

   ![The Delete Account button in the Danger Zone section of the Account Panel](2026-08-09-22-57-19.png)

3. Read the message, type `DELETE` in the confirmation box, and click **Delete Account**.

Your account is now scheduled for deletion. After 30 days, your documents, folders and images are
permanently erased and cannot be recovered.

## Changing your mind

Sign in at any point during those 30 days. The Account Panel opens automatically with a banner
telling you the deletion date. Click **Restore Account** and everything carries on as before.

> **Tip:** If you want to keep a copy of your work, open each document, select all of the text,
> and use **Copy as Markdown** on the formatting toolbar before you delete anything.
