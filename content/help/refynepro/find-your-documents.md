Once you have more than a handful of documents, searching beats scrolling.

## Steps

1. Click Document Search at the top of the Document Panel and start typing. Results appear as you
   type.
2. Read the results. Folders are listed first, then documents, and each one shows the folder path
   it lives in.

   ![Search results in the Document Panel showing matching documents with their folder paths](2026-08-09-21-17-20.png)

3. Click a result to open it, or click the pin icon beside it to **Reveal in folder** — this
   clears the search, expands the tree and shows you where the item actually sits.

Search matches **document titles, folder names and tags**. It does not search the text inside
your documents.

## Getting back to recent work

Click **Recent** in the Document Shortcuts to see the last 20 documents you changed,
most recent first. Search is switched off in this view — that is why the box reads
**Search disabled in Recent**.

> **Tip:** Untitled documents appear as **Untitled** and are hard to find later. Naming a document
> when you create it saves time.
