There is no save button and no **Cmd+S**. RefynePro saves your document about five seconds
after you stop typing.

## Steps

1. Write as normal.
2. Look at the status next to the document title to see where things stand:

   | Status | Meaning |
   |---|---|
   | **Unsaved** | You have made changes that are not stored yet. |
   | **Saving...** | The save is in progress. |
   | **✓ Saved** | Everything is stored. |

   ![The document header showing the Saved status next to the title](2026-08-09-13-14-59.png)

3. Before closing the tab, check that it reads **✓ Saved**.

## If you switch to another document while the current document status is still "Unsaved"

Switching to another document inside RefynePro is always safe — your current document is saved
first.

![](2026-08-09-13-40-48.png)

## If you close the browser too early

If you close the tab or window while changes are still unsaved, your browser will warn you and
ask whether you really want to leave. The exact wording comes from your browser, not from
RefynePro. Choose to stay, wait for **✓ Saved**, then close.

![](2026-08-09-13-28-23.png)



> **Tip:** A document must be under 100 KB of text. If you go over, a message tells you the size
> and the save is refused. Split very long documents in two.
