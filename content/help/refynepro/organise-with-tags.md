Tags let you group things that sit in different folders — everything for one client, say, or
everything still in draft.

## Steps

1. Open a document. Below the title you will find a box reading **Add tags...**
2. Type a tag and press **Enter** or type a comma. Repeat for as many tags as you need.

   ![The tag box below a document title, with two tags added](2026-08-09-21-24-39.png)
   
3. To remove a tag, click the **×** on it, or press **Backspace** when the box is empty.

As you type, RefynePro suggests tags you have used before. Picking a suggestion keeps your tags
consistent, which matters when you filter by them later.

## Filtering by tag

Click **Tags** in the Document Shortcuts to see every tag you have used. Click tags to
filter. With two or more selected, a **Match** control appears so you can choose between showing
items with *all* of the selected tags or *any* of them.

**Other ways to do this:** folders can be tagged too. Open a folder in the Workspace and use the
tag box beside its name.
