There are two ways to move a document: pick a destination from a menu, or drag it. Both work
only in the Document Tree.

## Steps

1. Right-click the document in the Document Tree and choose **Move**.

   ![Right click and choose "Move"](2026-08-09-18-00-57.png)

2. Every folder you can move it into now shows **← Click to move here**. Click your destination.

   ![The Document Tree in move mode, with Click to move here shown next to a folder](2026-08-09-18-05-00.png)

3. To take the document out of all folders instead, click the **Move to Root (No folder)** banner
   at the top of the Document Panel.

A message confirms the move and offers **Undo** if you picked the wrong folder.

**Other ways to do this:** drag the document onto a folder in the Document Tree and drop it.

![Drag and drop document(s) to move](2026-08-09-17-57-23.png)

## Moving several at once

Hold **Cmd** (Mac) or **Ctrl** (Windows) and click each document to select it, or click the first
and **Shift**-click the last to select a range. Then right-click and choose **Move 3 documents**,
where the number matches your selection.

> **Tip:** Select **Cancel** next to the document if you change your mind while choosing a
> destination.
