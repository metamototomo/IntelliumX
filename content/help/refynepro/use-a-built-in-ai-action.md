RefynePro can rewrite any text you select. There are five built-in actions: **Improve**,
**Summarise**, **Simplify**, **Professional** and **Casual**. Nothing changes in your document
until you decide to keep the result.

## Steps

1. Select the text you want to change. The toolbar appears after about a second, or press
   **Cmd+K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows) to show it straight away.
2. Click **AI Tools**, then choose an action.

   ![The AI Tools menu listing Improve, Summarise, Simplify, Professional, Casual and Custom](2026-08-09-17-40-08.png)

3. Read the result, then choose one of the three buttons:
   - **Replace** — swap your selected text for the new version.
   - **Insert Below** — keep your original and add the new version underneath it.
   - **Discard** — close the window and change nothing.

   ![The result window with the Discard, Insert Below and Replace buttons](2026-08-09-17-37-58.png)

The bottom of the result window shows which model ran, how many tokens it used, and how many
credits it cost. If the model explains its thinking, a **View Reasoning** link appears above that.

> **Important:** Credits are charged as soon as the result appears. **Discard** still uses them,
> because the AI has already done the work.

## If you cannot use AI Tools

- The button reads **Limit Reached** when you have used all your credits for the month.
- The toolbar does not appear on a locked document. Unlock it first.
- AI actions are not available while your cursor is inside a table.
