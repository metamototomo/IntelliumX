Favouriting a document puts it in a list of its own, so you do not have to remember which folder
it is in.

## Steps

1. Open the document.
2. Click the **star** icon next to the title. Its tooltip reads **Add to Favourites**.

   ![The star icon beside a document title in the Document Header](2026-08-09-21-41-38.png)

3. To see your favourites, click **Favourites** in the Document Shortcuts.

Favourited documents also show a small star beside them in the Document Tree, so you can spot
them in the normal folder hierarchy.

To remove one, open it and click the star again — the tooltip now reads **Remove from Favourites**.

> **Tip:** The Favourites list is ordered by when you last changed each document, so whatever you
> are working on now stays near the top.
