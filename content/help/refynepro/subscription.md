Your plan sets how many credits you get each month and how many documents you can keep.

## The plans

| | Free | Standard | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $2.99 per month | $6.99 per month |
| Credits | 3,000 per month | 100,000 per month | 500,000 per month |
| Documents | Up to 50 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Two-factor authentication | — | Yes | Yes |

## Checking your plan

Click the **person** icon in the Header to open the Account Panel. **Subscription** shows your
plan name, its status, and for paid plans the date it next renews.

## Upgrading

1. Open the Account Panel and click **Upgrade Plan**, or go to the Pricing page directly.
2. Click **Upgrade Now** on the plan you want.
3. Complete payment on the secure checkout page.

Your new allowance is available as soon as payment goes through.

## Changing or cancelling

Open the Account Panel and click **Manage Subscription**. This opens the secure billing portal,
where you can switch plans, update your card, view invoices or cancel.

If you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for — the Account
Panel changes to show **Expires on** and the date. After that you return to the Free plan.
