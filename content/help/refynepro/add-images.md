Images go into a document by pasting. Once pasted, they are uploaded and stored with your
document automatically.

## Steps

1. Copy an image — from a screenshot, a web page, or a file on your computer.
2. Click where you want it in your document and paste with **Cmd+V** (Mac) or **Ctrl+V**
   (Windows).

   ![An image pasted into a document](2026-08-09-13-13-23.png)

3. Click the image once to resize it. Pick one of the preset widths or enter your own, then click
   **Apply**.

Double-click any image to view it full screen. Press **Esc** to come back.

## What you can paste

JPEG, PNG, WebP and GIF files up to **4 MB**. Larger images are compressed automatically before
upload, and very large ones may still be refused with a message asking you to reduce the size
first.

> **Tip:** Dragging an image file into the page does not work — it has to be pasted. Open the
> file, copy it, then paste.
