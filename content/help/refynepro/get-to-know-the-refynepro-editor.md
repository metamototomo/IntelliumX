The RefynePro Editor has four main areas: the Header, Document Panel, Workspace and Side Panel.
This guide uses these names throughout.

![The RefynePro Editor showing the Header, Document Panel, Workspace and Side Panel](2026-08-08-21-57-29.png)

## Header

The **theme** icon switches between **Light Mode** and **Dark Mode**. The **gear** icon opens the AI Settings Panel. The **person** icon opens the Account Panel.

## Document Panel

The Document Panel helps you find and organise your work. It contains:

- **Document Search** — search document titles, folder names and tags.
- **Document Shortcuts** — switch between **Folder view**, **Favourites**, **Recent**, **Tags** and **Trash**.
- **Document Tree** — browse your folders and documents as a hierarchy.

## Workspace

Selecting a folder in the Workspace shows its contents and the **New Folder** and **New Document**
controls. Selecting a document shows two areas:

- **Document Header** — the document title, tags, favourite and lock controls, and save status.
- **Editor** — the page where you write, format text, add content and use AI actions.

## Side Panel

The Side Panel opens on the right when you select the **AI Settings Panel** or **Account Panel** icon in the Header:

- **AI Settings Panel** — choose the **AI Model**, apply **Presets**, adjust **Parameters** and
  review **Credit Usage**.
- **Account Panel** — manage account-related controls, including your plan, credits and security
  settings.
