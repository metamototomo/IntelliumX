RefynePro offers twelve AI models. They differ in quality, speed and how many credits they use,
so it is worth choosing the model that suits the job.

## Steps

1. With a document open, click the **gear** icon in the Header. Its tooltip reads
   **AI Settings Panel**.
2. Pick a model from the dropdown. They are grouped into **Budget**, **Balanced** and
   **Premium**.

   ![The AI Settings Panel with the model dropdown open](2026-08-09-22-29-06.png)

3. Close the panel. Your choice applies to every AI action from now on.

Not sure which to pick? Click **Compare** next to the dropdown for a guide to what each model is
good at.

## Adjusting the response

Below the model you will find three presets — **Creative**, **Balanced** and **Precise** — which
are the quickest way to change the character of the answers. **Creative** gives more varied
wording, **Precise** sticks closer to your original.

Underneath those are individual sliders for **Max Tokens**, **Temperature**, **Top P** and
**Top K**. Hover over any of them for an explanation. Some models ignore some of these, and the
panel tells you when that is the case.

> **Tip:** Budget models cost fewer credits per action. If you are running low, switching to one
> for routine edits stretches your allowance a long way.

> **Note:** Model and slider settings are remembered by this browser, not by your account. On
> another computer you will need to set them again.
